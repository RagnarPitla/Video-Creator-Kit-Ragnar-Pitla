Layer 5
Experience
Where the human meets it. If they have to learn a new place to go, they will not go.
In the ERPNot a side chat window
In TeamsWhere approvals already live
In emailFor the people who never left
ScheduledNo human present at all
Layer 4
Orchestration
Routing, retries and handoff. One parent, several narrow children, no giant agent.
RouterDescription-based, not keyword
Child agentsOne job each, testable alone
HandoffEscalates with the reason attached
RetryBounded, and it says it retried
Layer 3
Policy
The rules, in rows, owned by the business. This is the layer that makes the rest auditable.
TolerancesVersioned, with an author
AuthorityWho may commit what, and up to what
Refusal rulesWhen to abstain instead of guess
AuditWhich rule fired on every answer
Layer 2
Access
Typed tools over the ERP. Read is generous, write is narrow and logged both ways.
MCP toolsTyped in, typed out
Read scopeBroad, cheap, cached
Write scopeNarrow, approved, reversible
IdentityActs as someone, not as root
Layer 1
Ground truth
The records themselves, with a freshness guarantee. Everything above inherits this layer's mistakes.
Master dataOne source, dated
TransactionsThe ledger, not a copy of it
DocumentsChunked with their metadata
Freshness SLAA number, not a hope
Read it upward Nobody buys layer one, and every failure in layers two to five is really a layer one problem wearing a costume.
