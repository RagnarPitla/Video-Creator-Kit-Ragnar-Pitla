# In Our AI Era — Visual Style Guide

## Brand Overview

"In Our AI Era" uses a **flat editorial illustration style** that feels warm, smart, and approachable — not corporate tech stock art. Think: bold editorial illustration meets podcast energy. Every image has personality.

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm Cream | `#FFF8F0` | All image backgrounds |
| Primary | Coral Red | `#D93A52` | Hero elements, show title, key highlights |
| Secondary | Dark Navy | `#1A1A2E` | Bold outlines, text, shadows |
| Accent 1 | Soft Pink | `#F5A8B8` | Decorative elements, speech bubble borders |
| Accent 2 | Light Sky | `#D6EEFF` | Speech bubble fills, info callouts |
| Accent 3 | Warm Yellow | `#FFD166` | Highlight stars, key term emphasis |
| Neutral | Light Gray | `#EDEDF0` | Subtle backgrounds, secondary boxes |
| White | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Inner fills, speech bubble backgrounds |

**Never use:** harsh pure black (#000000) for fills. Use dark navy (#1A1A2E) for outlines. No gradients. No photorealism.

---

## Illustration Style

- **Line weight:** Bold black/navy outlines (3-5px equivalent), consistent across all elements
- **Fills:** Flat solid colors — no gradients, no textures, no patterns
- **Shadows:** None on main elements. Optional: a tiny offset shadow in coral/pink for depth on floating elements
- **Corner radius:** Rounded everywhere — speech bubbles, boxes, character features
- **Feel:** Approachable and warm. Editorial illustration, not corporate infographic

## Character Reference

### Ragnar (Male Host — Left)
- **Skin:** Deep warm brown
- **Hair:** Natural curly/afro, dark brown-black, medium volume
- **Build:** Tall, athletic-ish, confident posture
- **Outfit:** Dark navy or charcoal blazer/jacket over a darker inner layer. Casual professional.
- **Expression:** Engaged, slightly leaning forward, builder energy — "let me show you how this works"
- **Position:** Usually on the LEFT side of the composition
- **Microphone:** Standing mic or handheld, near mouth
- **Style notes:** Bold outlines, flat fills on clothing, minimal facial detail but expressive

### Tina (Female Host — Right)
- **Skin:** Fair/light, warm undertone
- **Hair:** Light blonde or warm light brown, shoulder length or slightly longer, relaxed/natural
- **Build:** Medium height, poised, engaged body language
- **Outfit:** Coral/red jacket or blazer — this is her signature color, matches brand primary
- **Expression:** Curious, strategic thinker — "I've been researching this" energy
- **Position:** Usually on the RIGHT side of the composition
- **Microphone:** Standing mic or handheld, near mouth
- **Style notes:** Bold outlines, her jacket should always read as coral/red to match brand

### Both Characters Together
- Seated at a rounded table/desk (curved modern table shape)
- Equal visual weight — neither dominates
- Warm chemistry — facing slightly toward each other or the viewer
- Same art style, same line weight — they belong in the same universe

## Decorative Elements

| Element | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Stars** | 6-point outlined stars (not filled), in coral or soft pink | Scattered around composition, 3-6 per image |
| **Dot clusters** | Small 3-5 dot groupings, same color family | Corner accents, transitions |
| **Sparkles** | 4-point or asterisk sparkle shapes | Near key terms, highlights |
| **Speech bubbles** | Rounded rectangles with tail, light sky blue fill, navy outline | Floating AI concepts around characters |
| **Pill labels** | Rounded rectangle badges with text | Tech term callouts, agent names |
| **Floating boxes** | Small rounded boxes with labels | Architecture nodes, concept labels |

---

## Typography Style (for prompts)

- **Show title:** "IN OUR AI ERA" — bold, chunky, stacked, coral/red fill with dark navy outline. Mix of uppercase, slightly playful — not mechanical
- **Host tagline:** "with Ragnar and Tina" — smaller, clean, dark navy, handwritten or clean sans-serif
- **Tech labels:** Bold sans-serif, dark navy or white on colored pill/box. Clean, readable at distance
- **No cursive** except for signature-style elements. Keep text readable and direct.

## Architecture Diagrams — Brand Adaptation

When creating architecture diagrams in brand style, adapt as follows:

### Instead of corporate flat boxes:
Use **illustrated floating nodes** — rounded boxes with bold navy outlines, flat color fills from brand palette, with small illustrative icons inside

### Color mapping for architecture:
| Node Type | Color |
|---|---|
| Orchestrator / Main Agent | Coral Red `#D93A52` |
| Connected Agent | Dark Navy `#1A1A2E` with white text |
| Child Agent | Soft Pink `#F5A8B8` with navy text |
| Tool / MCP / Integration | Light Sky `#D6EEFF` with navy text |
| Data Store | Warm Yellow `#FFD166` with navy text |
| User / External | White `#FFFFFF` with navy border |

### Arrows and connections:
- Bold, slightly curved connectors — not straight mechanical lines
- Arrow style: friendly rounded arrowheads, coral or navy
- Labels on arrows in small pill badges

### Characters in architecture diagrams:
- Optional: small character silhouettes in corner "explaining" the diagram
- Or: just the illustrated node style without characters (cleaner for pure technical use)

### Decorative integration:
- 2-3 outlined stars in corners
- Dot clusters at transitions
- Overall feel: "illustrated tech explainer from an editorial magazine" not "enterprise whitepaper"

---

## What to Avoid

- No photorealism or stock photo style
- No 3D rendering
- No harsh shadows or bevels
- No overcrowded layouts — whitespace matters
- No inconsistent character designs between images (establish and lock via Nano Banana Visual DNA)
- No generic clipart icons
- No corporate sterile color palettes (Microsoft blue defaults, etc.)
- Never break the character consistency — Ragnar is always dark-skinned with curly hair, Tina always has the coral jacket
