Where does it start?An agent with no declared entry point picks one for you, and picks differently tomorrow.
How far does it reach?One hop or three. Most teams never decide, so the retriever decides.
What tells it to stop?With no stopping rule the loop ends when the budget does.
Who reads the trace?If only the author can follow it, it is not a trace. It is a log.
What happens on failure?Undefined is an answer, and it is the one you get at 2am.
1  Declare the entry
Name the one place a request may begin. Everything downstream is reachable from it, or it is dead code.
single doortyped inputauth at the edge
2  Bound the traversal
Depth, breadth and permitted edges, written down. A bigger context window does not fix an unbounded walk. It makes being wrong more expensive.
max depthedge allowlisttoken ceiling
3  State the stop condition
Answer found, budget spent, or confidence below floor. Pick one, make it observable, and alert when it changes.
confidence floorstep captimeout
4  Emit the trace
The path taken, not just the answer. A result you cannot replay is a result you cannot defend in a review.
decision logtool callsinputs hashed
5  Define the fallback
What it does when it cannot answer. Silence and a confident guess are both failures; only one of them is visible.
escalateabstainhuman handoff
Certified pathA smaller declared path beats a large accidental one every time.
Permissions travelThe agent knows what it may do before it acts, not after.
Replay is the testIf you cannot re-run the decision, you are not debugging. You are guessing.
Cost is a signalA step that got expensive usually got wrong first.
Abstaining ships"I do not know" is a valid, testable output.
